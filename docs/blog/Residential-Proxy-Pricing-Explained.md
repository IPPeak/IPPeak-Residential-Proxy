# Residential Proxy Pricing Explained: How to Choose the Right Plan Without Overspending

Residential proxy pricing mainly depends on the billing method, IP resources, traffic requirements, and usage period. Before choosing a plan, determine whether the business needs rotating IPs, fixed IPs, or unlimited traffic, then compare prices instead of focusing only on the starting rate.

<img src="https://i.postimg.cc/RhLqzx9T/Residential-Proxy-Pricing-Explained.png" alt="Residential Proxy Pricing Explained" width="1000">

## 1. What Are the Different Residential Proxy Billing Models?

**Pay-as-you-go by traffic:** Pay per GB. Suitable for tasks with predictable traffic needs or irregular usage.

**Time-based billing:** Purchase access by the hour, day, or month. Ideal for ongoing tasks and high-traffic workloads.

**IP-based billing:** Pay according to the number of IPs and the usage period. Suitable for businesses that require fixed IP addresses.

## 2. What Factors Affect Residential Proxy Pricing?

- **IP resources:** The size of the IP pool, resource selection standards, and network performance can all affect pricing.
- **Geographic coverage:** If multiple countries or regions or specific ASNs are required, check whether the plan supports them.
- **Traffic and bandwidth:** For high-traffic tasks, compare the total cost and available bandwidth rather than looking at the unit price alone.
- **IP usage requirements:** Whether the task requires frequently rotating IPs or consistently using fixed IPs directly affects which product is most suitable.

## 3. How to Choose a More Cost-Effective Residential Proxy

**Low traffic and a limited budget:** Choose a pay-per-GB plan. Estimate actual traffic requirements first to avoid purchasing more resources than necessary.

**Multi-region data collection:** Consider premium residential proxies. If the business involves multiple countries, regions, or ASNs, prioritize IP coverage, pool size, and connection performance.

**High traffic and continuous operations:** Consider unlimited residential proxies. Check whether the available bandwidth meets task requirements and which billing period—hourly, daily, or monthly—offers the best value.

**Fixed IP requirements:** Choose static residential proxies. If the business requires IPs that do not rotate and a consistent connection environment, prioritize static residential proxies and confirm the required locations and IP quantity.
