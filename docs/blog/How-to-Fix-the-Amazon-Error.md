# How to Fix the Amazon “Sorry, Something Went Wrong on Our End” Error

When accessing Amazon, users may encounter the **“Sorry, Something Went Wrong on Our End”** error. Common symptoms include product pages failing to load, unusual search results, inaccessible shopping carts, and difficulties logging in or completing purchases. This message generally indicates that Amazon could not process a request successfully. However, the issue may not necessarily originate from Amazon's servers. Network connectivity, browser cache, IP addresses, and unusual request patterns can also contribute to the problem.

## 1. Why Does Amazon Show the Something Went Wrong Error?

Before troubleshooting, it is important to understand the potential causes of this error.

**(1) Temporary Amazon Server Issues**

When Amazon experiences server outages, maintenance, or temporary traffic overload, some page requests may fail to process correctly. These issues may affect multiple pages or users and generally require waiting for the service to recover.

**(2) Unstable Network Connections**

Network latency, DNS resolution failures, and unstable Wi-Fi connections can interrupt requests and prevent Amazon pages from loading properly.

**(3) Browser Cache and Cookie Conflicts**

Expired cookies, corrupted cache files, or browser extensions may interfere with page loading and login sessions, causing requests to fail.

**(4) IP Address or Request Anomalies**

Sending a large number of requests through the same IP address within a short period, or using shared IPs with poor network quality, may increase the likelihood of access failures or additional verification challenges.

## 2. How to Fix the Amazon Something Went Wrong Error

Follow these troubleshooting steps to identify and resolve the issue.

**(1) Refresh the Page and Check Amazon Service Status**

Start by reloading the page and trying again after a few minutes. If multiple Amazon pages are inaccessible, check service status updates or relevant outage reports to determine whether Amazon is experiencing platform-side issues.

**(2) Clear Browser Cache and Cookies**

Open your browser's privacy settings and clear the cache and cookies associated with Amazon. Then log in again.

You can also try accessing Amazon in incognito mode. If the website works normally in this mode, existing browser data or extensions may be causing conflicts.

**(3) Check Your Network Connection and Switch Networks**

Visit other websites to determine whether your current network is working properly. If connection problems persist, restart your router or switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data to test the connection.

**(4) Check Your IP Address and Proxy Configuration**

If Amazon works normally after switching networks, the original network environment or IP address may require further investigation. You should also verify your proxy server address, port, authentication credentials, and protocol settings to rule out configuration errors.
