# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone who participates in this project.

## Expected Behavior

We expect all contributors and community members to:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Communicate clearly and constructively.
- Accept different opinions and approaches.
- Focus on improving the project.
- Provide useful and constructive feedback.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks or insults.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private or sensitive information without permission.
- Any other behavior that makes the community unsafe or unwelcoming.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it through the appropriate project communication channel or contact the project maintainers.

Reports will be reviewed and handled appropriately.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take appropriate action in response to behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, including removing content or restricting participation in the project.

Thank you for helping keep this community respectful and welcoming.
